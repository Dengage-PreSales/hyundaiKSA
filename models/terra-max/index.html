Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go (SCC with S&G)

Helps maintain a safe distance from the front vehicle at a set speed, automatically adjusting (stopping/starting) in response to the front vehicle's movement.

Lane Keeping Assist (LKA)

LKA warns the driver if leaving a lane without signaling. If unintentional lane departure is detected, it helps resteer the vehicle to safety.

Blind Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist (BCA)

BCA warns you of any vehicle in the rear blind spot when changing lanes or exiting parking spaces, and applies emergency braking if collision risk is detected.

Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist 1.5 (FCA 1.5)

Monitors your surroundings and warns you if there is a risk of collision, and automatically assists with emergency braking if the risk of collision increases.

Electronic Stability Control (ESC)

Electronic Stability Control helps prevent skidding and loss of control during sudden maneuvers, keeping the SUV steady and secure.

Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)

TPMS displays live pressure for each tire for real-time tire awareness, helping you maintain safety, efficiency, and stability.

Parking Distance Warning (PDW) - Forward/Reverse

PDW warns against collision with objects around the vehicle when moving at low speeds, with proximity sensors in front and rear bumpers that detect obstacles.

Downhill Brake Control (DBC)

DBC automatically manages braking on steep descents, keeping speed controlled without pedal input.

Electronic Parking Brake (EPB)

EPB engages with a button and auto-releases for smooth takeoff, adding convenience and safety.

Electrochromic Rear-View Mirror (ECM)

The ECM dims harsh headlight glare from behind, improving visibility and reducing driver fatigue.

6 Airbag System

The 6-airbag system provides all-round impact protection for all passengers, enhancing safety.

Child Seat Anchor

ISOFIX anchors ensure safe, stable installation of compatible child seats, for secure family travel.

Everyday Convenience

Dual Zone AC with Air Purification

Fully automatic climate control lets driver and passenger set independent temperatures. The cluster ionizer purifies the cabin, creating cleaner, fresher air on every journey.

Rear AC Vents for All Rows

Dedicated vents for second and third rows ensure true 3-row comfort, giving every passenger proper airflow and cooling, for better journeys

Wireless Charger (Front Row)

Fast, cable-free charging for front-row passengers keeps devices powered and clutter-free.

Rear Manual Curtain

Built-in rear manual curtains provide added privacy and sun protection for back-seat passengers when needed.

Push-Button Start with Smart Key

Enter, start, and drive with ease using the smart key and push-button ignition.

Voice Command & Bluetooth

Hands-free calling, voice actions, and seamless audio connectivity keep your attention on the road, while enjoying your trip.

Type-C USB Charging Ports

Multiple Type-C ports deliver quick charging for multi across all rows.
