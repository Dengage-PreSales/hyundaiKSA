Bold SUV Design. Commanding Presence

Panoramic Driver Display

Drive & Terrain Modes

Switch between Drive and Terrain modes to adapt to changing road conditions, optimizing control, grip, and stability on every journey.

Safety
Built for Safer Journeys

Rear View Monitor (RVM)

Displays a live rear camera feed when reversing, enhancing visibility behind the vehicle to detect obstacles and improve overall maneuvering confidence.

Parking Distance Warning – Forward & Reverse

Uses front and rear sensors to detect nearby objects at low speeds, providing alerts that help you park with greater precision and avoid minor collisions.

Electric Parking Brake with Auto Hold

Provides convenient one touch parking and automatically holds the vehicle at stops, enhancing safety and ease in traffic.

Day & Night Rear View Mirror

Reduces glare from vehicles behind, improving night visibility and enhancing driving comfort and safety.

Reinforced Body Structure

Reinforced body structure enhances crash protection and rigidity, impact absorption, cabin strength and overall stability for safer drives.

6 Airbags

6 Airbags includes front, side, and curtain airbags designed for controlled deployment, enhancing protection for occupants during collisions.

Everyday Made Easier

Smart connectivity, intuitive controls and thoughtful features enhance comfort and convenience in every drive.

Automatic AC temperature control

Automatic AC temperature control maintains your preferred cabin setting with ease. The clear digital display shows current settings, allowing precise adjustments for consistent comfort in every drive.

Wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™

Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto let you connect your smartphone seamlessly without cables. Access calls, music, navigation and apps directly on the display for a more intuitive and connected driving experience.

Front Row Ventilated Seats

Front ventilated seats circulate air through the seat surfaces to help maintain a comfortable temperature for the driver and front passenger, enhancing comfort during hot weather and longer journeys.

Bluelink Connected Car Services

Bluelink lets you remotely control air conditioning, door locks, windows, and heated seats, monitor vehicle status and battery health, and receive real-time safety and speed alerts, all from your smartphone. *Available in selected countries only

2nd-row manual curtains

2nd-row manual curtains provide added privacy and sun protection, keeping rear passengers comfortable on every family journey.

USB Type‑C Charging Ports

The first row USB Type C ports provide charging and connectivity, with one port capable of switching between data transfer and charging modes, offering flexibility for connected devices and in car technology.

Wireless Charger

The wireless phone charger, located below the AC controls, enables effortless charging of compatible smartphones without the need for cables, keeping the cabin tidy and clutter free.

Smart Key

The Smart Key with Push Button Start enables effortless entry and ignition, while the built in immobilizer enhances security by preventing unauthorized engine start ups.
