Smart Cruise Control (SCC)

Helps maintain distance from the front vehicle, drive at a set speed, and automatically reacts (stops/starts) depending on the front vehicle's movement.

High Beam Assist (HBA)

High Beam Assist (HBA) automatically adjusts the high beams by detecting ambient light and illuminance, such as the lamps of oncoming or preceding vehicles.

Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist (RCCA)

While reversing, RCCA warns you of any risk of collision with oncoming vehicles on any side. Brakes are automatically applied if the collision becomes imminent.

Reverse Parking Collision-Avoidance Assist (PCA)

PCA warns you if there is a risk of collision with a rear object when reversing. Brakes are automatically applied when the collision becomes imminent.

Blind Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist (BCA)

BCA warns you of any rear side vehicle in the blind spot, when changing lanes or exiting parking, and applies emergency braking if a collision risk is detected.

Lane Following Assist 2 (LFA 2)

Helps center the vehicle in the lane. Also, with a direct Hands-On Detection (HOD) sensor, it determines whether the driver is holding the steering.

Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist 2 (FCA 2)

FCA 2 warns and assists you with braking when a risk of collision is detected with vehicles, pedestrians, or bicycles, helping reduce the chance of accidents.

Driver Attention Warning (DAW)

DAW keeps you alert by monitoring driver focus, suggesting breaks when attention is low, and signaling when the car ahead moves after a stop.

Blind Spot View Monitor (BVM)

Activating the turn signal displays the rear-side image of the corresponding direction in the cluster, enhancing driver safety and awareness.

Surround View Monitor (SVM)

SVM displays the surrounding view while driving and parking, from 4 wide view cameras. You can also send SVM images to your smartphone for remote monitoring.

Convenience Perfected

From the moment you step inside, SANTA FE surrounds you with intuitive comfort that adapts to your every need.

UV-C Sterilization Tray

The first-of-its-kind UV-C Sterilization Tray is applied to the top of the passenger glove compartment, providing easy sterilization of frequently used items, such as cell phones, wallets, etc.

Dual Full Auto Air Conditioner

Adjusts airflow based on temperature settings for more comfort, while sensors detect and automatically improve air quality. Comes with a fine dust sensor, auto defog system, air cleaning mode, and an after-blow function.

Dual Wireless Charger

The Dual Wireless Charger powers two devices simultaneously, keeping you connected with effortless convenience.

Switch-Type USB-C Module

Enjoy faster charging and seamless connectivity with the switch-type USB-C module for added convenience.

BOSE Premium Sound System with 12 Speaker

The improved 12-speaker Bose Premium Sound System with new CenterPoint 360 technology has been tuned by Bose engineers to deliver a rich and immersive listening experience in every seat.

Remote Smart Parking Assist (RSPA)

Allows you to move the vehicle forward and backward when parking in tight locations, using the Smart Key buttons to control it.

Smart Power Tailgate

Hands-free convenience is provided by the Smart Power Tailgate, which opens automatically when you approach with the Smart Key.

Digital Center Mirror

The Digital Center Mirror transforms the traditional rearview mirror into a digital display, offering a wide rear view through a camera. It allows switching between standard and digital modes with adjustable brightness.

Hidden-Type C-Pillar Assist Handle

Elegantly integrated into the C-pillar, the hidden assist handle provides easy grip and refined functionality.
